Privacy

Last updated 27 November 2026

This is the personal site of Meesum Ahmed (lrxrn). It is built to know as little about you as possible.

What is collected

Nothing that identifies you. This site has no cookies, accounts, forms, advertising or tracking pixels, and no profiles of visitors are built.

Analytics

This site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless service that counts page views, country, referrer, device type and page speed in aggregate. It does not use cookies, does not identify visitors and does not track you across sites.

The analytics script is injected automatically by Cloudflare when pages are served, and it loads from static.cloudflareinsights.com, so Cloudflare sees connection details such as your IP address. Analytics are collected from visitors outside the EU only. For visitors in the EU, the script is not injected and nothing is collected. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for how it handles connection data.

Hosting and live status

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any host, GitHub sees your IP address and browser details in its own server logs. Those logs cannot be accessed or changed from this site. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The pill in the corner that shows what is being worked on or played connects to a small service on Cloudflare. It only receives the live connection, so Cloudflare sees connection details such as your IP address, as any host does. It is not used to identify or track you.
  • Fonts and all other files are served from this site, so no font or script provider sees you.

Stored in your browser

If you switch between light and dark, the choice is saved in your browser (localStorage) so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device and it is not a cookie.

If you send an email

Your message and address are kept only to reply. Ask, and they will be deleted.

Your data and deleting it

Because no personal data about visitors is kept, there is nothing to export or delete. For anything held by GitHub or Cloudflare, contact them directly.

Links to other sites

GitHub, LinkedIn, Discord and project links open other websites with their own privacy policies.
